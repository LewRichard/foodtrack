## What we collect

- **Account information**: your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).
- **Profile**: your name, username and weekly gym goal.
- **Check-ins**: the photos you take in the app, optional captions, and the date and time of each check-in.
- **Social data**: your friend list, friend requests, emoji reactions, blocks, and reports you submit.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or ads.

## How we use it

Only to provide the app: showing your check-ins to you and your accepted friends, calculating streaks,
and keeping the community safe (reviewing reports).

## Who can see your data

- Your check-in photos, captions and dates are visible **only to you and people you have accepted as friends**.
- Your name and username are visible to other signed-in users so they can find you and send friend requests.
- We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase ([supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)),
in [REGION].

## Reminders

Gym reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete individual check-ins at any time, and delete your
entire account in **Profile → Delete account**, which permanently removes your profile, photos, check-ins,
reactions and friendships.

## Children

collect their data.
