Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Operated by Lew Zi Qian

Summary

FoodTrack is a food diary. You sign in with an email address or a Google account, and your diary is stored in your account so it works on all your devices. When you use an AI feature (meal photos, describing a meal, menu scanning, the weekly check-in, the meal planner or chatting with Lew), the data that feature needs is sent to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude AI. If you join Community, what you post is shown to the people you choose. We don't sell your data, show ads or use it for advertising.

Your account

To use FoodTrack you sign in with your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash), or with Google. With Google, we receive your email address and the basic profile details Google shares (such as your name); we never see your Google password. Accounts are run by our database provider, Supabase.

Your diary

Your diary is stored in your account and on your device so the app also works offline. It includes the foods you log with their nutrition values, health scores and small meal thumbnails, water, exercise calories, weigh-ins, your name, goals, goal weight, language, and the body details you give when you start (sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal). Only you can access it when signed in.

Some things stay only on your device and are never uploaded: your conversations with Lew, your AI weekly check-ins and meal plans, your theme, and (in the iPhone app) step counts from Apple Health.

AI features

These features send data to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the result. Our server does not store what you send or what comes back. Anthropic processes requests under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

  • Meal photos: the photo (resized) and any note you type. You're asked for consent before the first photo is sent, and you can withdraw it in Profile → Photo scanning.
  • Menu scanning: the menu photos (resized), any preferences you type (e.g. "vegetarian"), your goal (lose, maintain or gain) and how many calories, protein, carbs and fat you have left that day.
  • Describing a meal: the text you type or dictate. If you use the microphone button, your browser's own speech recognition turns your voice into text (in some browsers this is done by the browser maker's service, such as Google or Apple); FoodTrack never receives audio.
  • Weekly check-in: a summary of your last 7 days (daily totals, the names and scores of logged meals, water, exercise calories, weigh-ins) and your goals.
  • Chat with Lew: your messages, your name, your body details and goals, and the same 7-day summary, so the answers fit you.
  • Meal planner: your goals, body details, the preferences you type, and the names of foods you log most often.

AI answers are estimates and general wellness information, not medical advice.

Free and Premium plans

To apply the free plan's daily limit, we count how many AI meal logs (photo, description or menu) each account makes per day. These counts are kept for 60 days. If you have Premium, we store when it ends and how it was purchased. Premium is sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record, which collects your payment details, billing address and email to process the payment and tax. Paddle tells us only the status of your subscription and its subscription and customer IDs; your card details never reach FoodTrack.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to our server, which looks it up in the public Open Food Facts database. The camera image stays on your device.

Community

Community is optional. If you join, we store your username, display name, profile photo, and what you create: posts (with an optional photo), comments, likes, messages, friend requests, blocks, reports and your logging streak.

  • Your username, display name and profile photo can be seen by other FoodTrack users.
  • Each post is shown either to your friends or to everyone signed in, as you choose when you write it. Its comments and likes are shown to the same people.
  • Messages can be read only by you and the person you send them to.
  • Your logging streak (how many days in a row you've logged a meal, and your best run) is shown to your friends on the streak leaderboard. It's shown to everyone only if you turn on "Show me on the global board".
  • Blocked people can't see your posts or message you. Reports are seen only by us, to review content that may break the community rules.

You can delete your posts and comments at any time.

Notifications

If you turn on meal reminders or Community notifications, we store your reminder times, your time zone and a push address for each device you allow, so our server can send notifications through your browser's or phone's push service. Turning notifications off, signing out or deleting your account removes the device's push address.

Apple Health

Where the iPhone app offers it, you can choose to connect Apple Health. FoodTrack then reads your step count, active (exercise) calories and body weight. Steps stay on your device. Exercise calories and weigh-ins are added to your diary like any other diary data. FoodTrack never writes to Apple Health, never uses Health data for advertising or marketing, and never sells it or shares it with third parties. You can stop it any time in Profile, and remove access in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Technical data

Like any website, our server and hosting provider receive your IP address with each request. We use it in memory only to limit how many AI requests one visitor can make per hour, and do not keep it. Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can export a copy of your diary at any time in Profile → Your data. You can delete your account in Profile → Account ("Delete account"): this immediately and permanently deletes your diary, Community profile, posts, comments, likes, messages, streak, photos and notification settings. Data kept only on your device is removed when you sign out, delete the app or clear the site's data.

Children

FoodTrack is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Health disclaimer

Nutrition estimates and AI answers are approximate and are not medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional before making major changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if our practices change. Questions or requests: support@foodtrack.fit.
