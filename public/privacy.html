Summary

FoodTrack keeps your food diary on your device. If you choose to create an account, your diary is also stored online so you can use it on several devices. When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to our server and on to an AI service to estimate its nutrition. We don't sell your data or show ads, and an account is optional.

Data stored on your device

Your diary entries, small meal thumbnails, goals, profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level), water log and access code are stored in your browser's or app's local storage. They stay on your device unless you export a backup or create an account (below). Deleting the app, clearing site data, or using "Reset all" in the Goals tab removes them.

If you create an account

Your email address, a securely hashed password, and your diary (logged foods with their nutrition values and small meal thumbnails, water, exercise calories, name, goals and body details) are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so they can be synced to your devices. Only you can access them when signed in. You can delete your account and all of this data at any time in the Profile tab ("Delete account"); deletion is immediate and permanent.

Meal reminders

If you turn on reminders, we store your chosen reminder times, your time zone, and a push address for each device you allow, so our server can send notifications. Turning reminders off, signing out, or deleting your account removes the device's push address.

Meal photos

When you choose to scan, the app sends the photo (resized) and any note you type to our server. The server forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the estimated foods and nutrients. Our server does not store photos or results after replying. Anthropic processes the request under its commercial terms and privacy policy. You're asked for consent before the first photo is sent, and you can withdraw it in the Goals tab.

Technical data

Like any website, our server and hosting provider receive your IP address with each request. We use it in memory only to limit how many scans one visitor can make per hour, and do not keep it. Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

Children

FoodTrack is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Health disclaimer

Nutrition estimates are approximate and are not medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional before making major changes to your diet.
