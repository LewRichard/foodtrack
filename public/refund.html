Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Operated by Lew Zi Qian

FoodTrack Premium is sold through our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com, which processes all payments and refunds.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with FoodTrack Premium, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment for a subscription, for any reason. For yearly plans, you can also ask for a refund within 14 days of each yearly renewal.

How to ask for a refund

  • Use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or contact Paddle at paddle.net; or
  • email us at support@foodtrack.fit with the email address you paid with.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time in Profile → Your plan → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Monthly renewals after the first 14 days are not refunded, unless required by law or the service didn't work as described.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country, such as a statutory right of withdrawal.
